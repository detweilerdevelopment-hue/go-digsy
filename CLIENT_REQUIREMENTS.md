# Client job posting — scope and delivery map

This is the one-page Owl trial authorized in the supplied project request. It is an enhancement of the original site, not the complete 30-page contract. The actual job posting is the source of this scope map.

## Exact trial quantities

- **1 enhanced existing story page:** Owl, accessible from root and its original adventure route.
- **3 independently separated moving regions:** 1 original owl and 2 original large leaves.
- **1 character greeting:** tap owl or its equivalent button for a short gentle lean. No blink is claimed.
- **2 ambient leaf motions**, with a tap-triggered rustle accessible on either leaf.
- **1 Find activity:** the original tiny wing leaf, with hint, success feedback, star celebration, keyboard activation, and replay.
- **1 selected-region coloring activity:** both large leaves share four choices (green, golden, coral, blue), preserving their original shading and outlines.
- Existing coloring-page toggle, larger text, full story, Wonder Together, and Look & Find retained.

## Practical full-project approach

Inventory all 30 source pages before issuing a fixed quote. Agree an exact per-page list of moving regions and activities; reuse the shared reader controls and activity state handling. Prepare only the elements that actually move. Prefer layered PSD/PSB, SVG, AI, layered illustration exports, or transparent PNG layers with matching background plates. Flattened WebP/PNG can be adapted through masks and background reconstruction, but preparation must be scoped page by page. Separately review the supplied four games and narration requirements. Use this working Owl trial as the animation-quality sample rather than promising a blink or identical effects on every future illustration.

No runtime AI or paid animation library is required. Hosting, domain, maintenance, full-project pricing, revision allowances, support duration and any future narration service costs must be agreed before the larger contract. Communication and payment remain on Upwork; no messages or financial commitments were sent on the user’s behalf.
